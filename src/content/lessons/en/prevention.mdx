## Brush gently and thoroughly

**Brush twice a day for about two minutes with fluoride toothpaste**, including before bed. Clean the outside, inside, and chewing surfaces of every tooth, including along the gums.

Spit out excess toothpaste. Do not immediately rinse with water, so fluoride stays on your teeth longer. Harder brushing is not better, and mouthwash does not replace brushing.

## Reach between teeth

Clean between teeth daily. Floss can help in tight spaces. Small brushes called **interdental brushes** can suit wider spaces.

A tool should fit comfortably; never force a brush through. Ask your dental practice to help you choose a size or show you how to use floss.

Plaque can irritate gums as well as damage teeth. If bleeding or swelling keeps happening, get advice.

## Make everyday choices easier

Choose water often, have less sugary food and drink, and avoid sipping acidic drinks for hours. Keep cleaning even if you rarely eat sweets: less sugar does not remove plaque.

Ask about a mouth that stays dry. Arrange dental checks at intervals agreed with your dentist; your needs may differ from a friend's. Do not wait for pain before getting a new change checked.

You do not have to do everything perfectly. Start with a routine you can repeat, and use the daily care guide for a reminder.
