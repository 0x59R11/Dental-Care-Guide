## Saliva does more than keep your mouth wet

Saliva helps wash away loose food and makes the mouth less acidic. It also supplies minerals that help keep teeth strong.

Teeth can lose minerals when acids attack them. Early weakened areas can sometimes take minerals back in. This helps explain why damage can improve before a hole forms.

## Why fluoride toothpaste helps

**Fluoride** helps strengthen enamel, the hard outer covering of teeth. It reduces mineral loss and helps early weakened areas recover minerals.

Brushing removes plaque; fluoride adds protection. Choose toothpaste with fluoride even if you already brush carefully.

## What it cannot do

Saliva and fluoride cannot fill an existing hole or regrow a broken piece of tooth. They cannot treat an infection either. Keep using fluoride toothpaste and ask a dentist about any suspected damage.

## If your mouth stays dry

Less saliva means less protection. Medicines and health conditions can sometimes cause dry mouth.

Drink water for comfort and ask a dentist or doctor about ongoing dryness. **Do not stop prescribed medicine on your own.** Finding the cause and getting advice matters even if your teeth do not hurt.
