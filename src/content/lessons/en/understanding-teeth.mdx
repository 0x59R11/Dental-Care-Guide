## The hard outside

The part of a tooth you see above the gum is the **crown**. The **roots** hold it in your jaw.

**Enamel** is the hard outer layer of the crown. It protects the tooth when you chew. It is strong, but acids can weaken it, and it can chip or crack.

## What is inside?

Under enamel is **dentin**, the layer that makes up much of the tooth. If dentin becomes exposed, hot or cold food may feel uncomfortable.

At the center is the **pulp**: soft tissue containing nerves and blood vessels. The layers around it help keep it safe. Deep decay or an injury can damage this living tissue.

## What can repair itself?

Early weakened enamel can sometimes regain minerals and become stronger. Saliva and fluoride help with this. But a missing piece of enamel cannot grow back, and toothpaste cannot fill a hole.

A sensitive tooth can have several causes. If discomfort keeps returning, ask a dentist to check it. You cannot tell how healthy a tooth is just by looking at its color.
